Raising Thinkers in the AI Era: A Nairobi Parent's Playbook

Parenting in the digital age Africa, a practical playbook for Nairobi families navigating screens, AI, and childhood.

African parents face a unique stack: mobile-first childhoods, M-Pesa economies, CBC reforms, and global AI arriving faster than any school policy. Parenting in the digital age Africa is not parenting in Silicon Valley, the tools, costs, and cultural expectations differ.

This playbook distils what we have learned from Nairobi families: think first, create boldly, use AI wisely.

No AI before thinking, child writes or draws their idea first.

Creation over consumption, for every hour of scrolling, one hour of making.

Talk about technology weekly, not lectures, conversations. What did you see? What do you think?
