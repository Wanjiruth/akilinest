What Are the Best AI Tools for My Child's Learning? A Parent's Shortlist

77% of parents are interested in AI-powered education tools. A curated, age-appropriate list for Nairobi families, and which tools to avoid.

Not every parent search is panic. 77% of parents are interested in AI-powered education tools. 75% are excited about their child learning through technology. You want the positive list, what actually helps.

The best educational AI tools share three traits: they require input from the child first, they produce visible output (a story, poster, presentation), and they work better with a parent or mentor nearby.

Canva AI and Bing Image Creator for illustrating stories the child wrote first.

Book Creator for publishing their own storybooks.

ElevenLabs (supervised) for giving characters voices, after the child created the characters.

Perplexity for challenging their own arguments with sourced answers.

Google Slides and CapCut for presenting what they learned.

Lovable and Figma (Builders stage) for turning ideas into real digital products.

Companion chatbots (Character.ai, Replika).

Any 'write my essay' one-click tool without a thinking step.

Unsupervised social platforms with AI filters marketed as child-safe without evidence.
