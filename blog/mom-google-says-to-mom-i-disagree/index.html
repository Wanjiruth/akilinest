From 'Mom, Google Says' to 'Mom, I Disagree': The AkiliNest Transformation

How children move from accepting AI answers to forming independent positions, a parent's perspective.

Stage one: 'Mom, Google says…', absolute trust. Stage two: 'Mom, I disagree with the AI because…', independent judgment. That transition is the AkiliNest transformation.

47% of parents are very concerned about kids getting inaccurate information from AI. The fix is not banning AI. It is teaching children to treat every AI output as a hypothesis to test.
